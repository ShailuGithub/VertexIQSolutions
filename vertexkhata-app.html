Every shop already keeps a running record of who paid, who owes, and what went out the door each day. VertexKhata keeps that same daily discipline and does the arithmetic, the printing and the syncing for you.

Whether you run one till or several branches, VertexKhata bills faster than a paper khata and never loses a page.

What's actually going wrong without it

Bills written out by hand
Slow at the counter, and totals only as accurate as the arithmetic.
No single day-end picture
Cash in, credit given, and expenses out — scattered across pages.
Branches out of sync
No shared view of customers or stock across more than one shop.
No control over staff access
Anyone with the register can see or change anything.

What you get

Billing & invoicing

Itemized bills in seconds — scan a barcode or pick from your product list, apply discounts, send to WhatsApp or print on a Bluetooth thermal printer.

Itemized expenses & day book

Log expenses the same way you bill. One page shows the whole day's cash in, credit given, and balance due.

Multi-branch ready

Set up sub-companies per branch under one account, with shared customers and products.

Staff logins

Give staff their own sign-in with exactly the rights you choose, from six separate permissions.

Cloud-synced or fully offline

Standard plans sync every branch in real time. A one-time Lifetime plan switches you to fully offline, local-first storage.

Secure by default

Sign in with Google — no passwords to manage — plus PIN or fingerprint app lock.
