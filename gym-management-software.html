If membership renewals, attendance and payments are tracked in a register, you're finding out about lapsed members and missed payments after the fact, not before. That's revenue quietly leaking out.

This software gives you a live view of every member's status, so renewals get followed up on time and nothing slips through.

What's actually going wrong without it

Missed renewal follow-ups
Members lapse quietly because nobody flagged the expiry date in time.
No attendance visibility
Can't tell who's actually using the gym versus who's about to churn.
Payments hard to track
Who's paid, who owes, and for which plan — scattered across a register.
No clear revenue picture
Hard to tell month-over-month whether membership revenue is growing or shrinking.

What you get

Renewal tracking & reminders

See who's due for renewal and get reminders before they lapse, not after.

Member records & attendance

Full membership history and check-in tracking, searchable whenever you need it.

Payment & plan tracking

Know exactly who's paid, who's pending, and which plan every member is on.

Revenue reports

A clear month-over-month picture of membership revenue, no manual adding-up required.
