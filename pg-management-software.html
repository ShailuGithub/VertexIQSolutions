/PG Management Software

Stop tracking rent in a register. Let the software do it.

Built for PG owners who are tired of chasing pending payments, forgetting which rooms are vacant, and losing tenant details in a notebook.

If you're still tracking rent, vacancies and tenant details in a register or Excel sheet, you already know how easily things slip through — a missed payment, a room you forgot was empty, a tenant's details you can't find when you need them.

This software replaces that manual process with one simple dashboard: who owes rent, which rooms are free, and every tenant's history, always up to date and always a glance away.

What's actually going wrong without it

Missed rent payments
No automatic reminder, so you find out weeks later.
Vacant rooms sitting empty
No live view of what's free, so leads go cold while you check manually.
Lost tenant records
ID proof, move-in dates, deposits — scattered across notebooks and phone notes.
No clear picture of income
Hard to tell, at a glance, what's collected and what's still pending.
